What the numbers mean

The 83 repositories hold 5,369 strategy classes, but only 2,982 of them are distinct. The rest are copies: the same strategy turns up in several collections, often unchanged. Unique counts how many strategies are credited to a repository as their first place of discovery — that is its real contribution.

The last column shows how many of a repository’s strategies have passed the checks so far. The number is strict: it requires a valid measurement, clean coverage and both bias checks. It is still growing while the expansion waves are worked through.

Collections with no contribution of their own

17 repositories contain nothing but copies

They are not worthless — they show how widely individual strategies are spread. For the analysis they do not count as independent observations, though; otherwise a much-copied strategy would multiply its own result. The largest example is keithorange/HUGE_FreqTrade_Strategy_Collection with 472 classes and not a single first find.
