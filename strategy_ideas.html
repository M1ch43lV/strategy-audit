Strategy ideas

One family per block: what the code does, read from the code, and what changed between revisions. Written by an LLM session, each sentence tied to the one revision it was read from - a family whose file has changed since is marked stale. This is a description, not a measurement, and it decides nothing.

The Date column is three different facts, each named: in the name is a date the author or the generator wrote into the class name, site is the published site's post date for the files taken from it, and repo is the last commit that touched the file in the repository it was harvested from - which is the revision's date where the repository has its own history and the date of an import where it does not (repo, import). No date here is the moment an author published a strategy unless the column says so. Dates are read from evidence/SOURCE_DATES.json; what is missing stays missing.

Zaratustra - 32 strategies in the corpus

One comparison decides everything: while RSI(7) sits above RSI(14) the strategy is long, while it sits below it is short, and each side is closed as soon as the two cross back. E0V1E - 39 strategies in the corpus

A 5m scalper whose entry is a bundle of conditions ORed together rather than one rule: EMA(8/16) against EMA/SMA(15) and RSI(4/14/20), the stochastic-fast fastk, and a Bollinger band around the typical price are collected into a list and reduced with `lambda x, y: x | y`, so any single member can open a trade. The exit side is where the work sits: a custom_stoploss that locks in above 1% profit and treats trades tagged 'ewo' differently, plus a custom_exit that reads the fastk and RSI columns of the current candle. minimal_roi and the 0.99 stoploss are nominal next to that.

Ichimoku - 31 strategies in the corpus

The Ichimoku cloud used as a filter around a Tenkan/Kijun cross rather than as the signal itself: the long needs the Tenkan line to cross above the Kijun line while the cloud is red, and the short is not the mirror but a different rule. Later revisions keep the same cross and read the cloud from a higher timeframe through informative_pairs, or replace the candle with its Heikin-Ashi version and compare that against the daily Senkou A/B - so the family is one idea read against different chart types.

GRIDDMIPRICEStrategyFuture - 7 strategies in the corpus

A grid, not a signal: the entry compares the close against an EMA threshold while +DI/-DI of an informative timeframe sit above an ADX level, both directions can be taken (can_short True), there is no exit signal at all (`exit_long = ()`), and the position is managed by a 97-line `adjust_trade_position` plus leverage(). The price levels the grid works on are set by custom_stake_amount in v7's 27-line version, so the file is a position manager that happens to have an entry condition.

Caveat: GRIDDMIPRICEStrategyFutureV7 is a degenerate strategy: its informatives and the DI/EMA calls are gone and `enter_long = dataframe['close'] > 0` enters whenever a candle closes - it looks like a hyperopt skeleton. The same corpus also holds it as a code-identical copy under the name ADX_PRICE_GRID_Strategy_Future_v7, one of the duplicate groups the corpus's own check reports.

MultiMA_TSL - 7 strategies in the corpus

A moving-average count instead of a moving-average cross: the buy needs a number of EMA and TRIMA conditions to hold at once (`base_nb_candles_buy_trima`, `base_nb_candles_sell_trima` and the matching counters are parameters), the sell mirrors it, and the custom_stoploss reads a copied sell column - `sell_copy`/`buy_copy` are written in populate_indicators so the exit can consult them without recomputing. A protections() block of 16 to 39 lines guards the position.

Caveat: MultiMA_TSL and MultiMA_TSL_618 extract to identical facts apart from one line, and MultiMA_TSL3 and MultiMA_TSL_461 are the same 687-line revision under two ids. Two of the seven strategies are therefore copies, and the canonical file of MultiMA_TSL5 is a repaired one under user_data/profile_repairs/.
