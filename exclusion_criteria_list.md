# Exclusion criteria

Generated by `exclusion_criteria.py`. Do not edit by hand.

A strategy is **excluded** when one of the criteria below holds on evidence this audit produced itself. Nothing else excludes a strategy. One that satisfies none of them is not excluded - it is unfinished, and `open_work` says what is missing.

Currently 118 of 900 strategies are excluded. The criteria are not exclusive - 5 strategies satisfy more than one, and the counts below therefore add up to more than the total.

## The criteria in full

### C1 &middot; Look-ahead bias found

**Machine test.** `lookahead == "FOUND" and lookahead_evidence == "native"`, together with `exclusion_basis == "own_measurement"`.

**What it means.** freqtrade's `lookahead-analysis` compared the strategy's signals against a run allowed to see the whole window, and the signals moved. The strategy is reading data it could not have had at the time.

**Why it is final.** This is the one defect no repair can undo without rewriting what the author wrote. A strategy that reads future candles has no measurable performance: every number it produces is a number about the future.

**What stands behind it.** The record names how many entries and exits of how many signals moved, and which indicators carry it. A verdict reached on fewer than ten signals does not count - the analyzer widens its window rather than ruling on too little.

**What this is not.** `lookahead_evidence` must read `native`. A FOUND carried over from the original sweep is not this criterion but an inherited claim, and the check has to be run here.

64 strategies, among them `ARIMA_15`, `AlexBTK_CT`, `AlexBattleTankKiller`, `AlexBattleTankKillerV3`, `AlexBattleTankKillerV40H`, `Auto_EI_t4c0s`.

### C2 &middot; Recursion bias found by our own ladder

**Machine test.** `recursive_evidence == "convergence:not_settled"`, together with `exclusion_basis == "own_measurement"`.

**What it means.** The warm-up ladder supplied 1, 2, 7, 14, 30, 90 and 365 days of history in turn, and at every rung at least one indicator still moved by 1.0 % or more against the full-history run. The indicator never settles, so its value depends on where the backtest happens to start.

**Why it is final.** Nothing downstream can be trusted: the same strategy on the same data gives different signals for a different start date. The ladder has already tried every warm-up worth trying, up to a full year of history.

**What stands behind it.** The record keeps the whole drift table - one column per rung, one row per indicator - so the finding can be read rather than taken on faith.

**What this is not.** `recursive_evidence` must start with `convergence:`. A FOUND from the baseline, or from a Wave B run under the parser that read the wrong column, is not this criterion. Two further things that are not findings: the analyzer printing no table while reporting no variance on any indicator, which is the strongest possible pass, and an indicator printed as `nan%` at every rung, which is a column the analyzer cannot express as a percentage at all.

52 strategies, among them `ARIMASTR`, `BBMod1`, `BB_RPB_TSL`, `BB_RPB_TSL_2`, `BB_RPB_TSL_BI`, `BB_RPB_TSL_BIV1`.

### C3 &middot; Ran the whole window and never traded

**Machine test.** `primary_reason == "no_trades_in_full_measurement" and trade_evidence == "full_window"`, together with `exclusion_basis == "own_measurement"`.

**What it means.** The strategy started, ran `20200301-20260821` across all eight pairs, and opened no position at all.

**Why it is final.** There is nothing to classify. Market-phase efficiency is a statement about trades, and this strategy makes none in six and a half years. Several are deliberate: a template, a demonstration of stop-loss handling, a strategy whose entry column is set to False in the source.

**What stands behind it.** Eight per-pair runs over the full window, each with its own archive and hash.

**What this is not.** `trade_evidence` must read `full_window`. Zero trades in the one-month trial run means nothing - the window is too short to expect any. And a strategy that trades nothing because it was given the wrong profile - a futures strategy run on spot, a short-only strategy with `can_short` unset - is a setup fault of ours, not this criterion.

7 strategies, among them `BreakEven`, `DoesNothingStrategy`, `Miku_PP_v3`, `MyStrategyTemplate`, `Obelisk_3EMA_StochRSI_ATR`, `ViN`.

## What does not exclude a strategy

Each of these has at some point moved strategies into `excluded` and has been withdrawn. They are listed so the mistake is not repeated.

### A trap found by reading the source

**What it is.** `traps.py` scans the source for the patterns the freqtrade community's Backtesting Traps article names: a trailing stop tighter than a realistic spread, a minimal ROI too tight for the timeframe, a declared stop of -0.99.

**Why it does not exclude.** It is a heuristic over source text, not a measurement. And what it describes is a fill-realism problem - the backtest assumes an order of events inside a candle that would not fill that way in the market - which inflates how much a strategy appears to earn. It says nothing about whether the strategy read the future.

**What it cost.** 40 strategies that run and trade were excluded on it, 35 of them without a look-ahead run of ours ever having happened.

**What happens instead.** The flag stays visible in `traps_n`, the strategy goes through both bias checks like everything else, and the realism problem is met where it lives - in the run, with `--timeframe-detail 1m`, which resolves the order of events inside a candle from real one-minute data instead of assuming it.

### A finding inherited from the original sweep

**What it is.** The frozen baseline carries look-ahead and recursion verdicts from a sweep that ran in an environment whose preconditions this audit never established.

**Why it does not exclude.** It is somebody else's measurement of a different setup. This audit does not issue a verdict on one.

**What it cost.** 15 rows sat under `own_measurement` on an inherited recursion FOUND, and not one of them had a ladder run of ours.

**What happens instead.** `exclusion_basis` reads `inherited`, the cohort reads `exclusion unconfirmed`, and the row carries the work that would settle it.

### A check that returned no verdict (NA)

**What it is.** The check ran and produced nothing to judge - too few trades, a crash, an output the reader could not parse.

**Why it does not exclude.** The absence of a result is not a result. Reading NA as a failure convicts a strategy of what was never measured.

**What happens instead.** `no_verdict_on_...` as the reason, and the check is queued again.

### The analyzer refusing for want of a declared warm-up

**What it is.** `recursive-analysis` will not start on a strategy whose `startup_candle_count` is zero, and says so.

**Why it does not exclude.** Nothing was compared. The refusal is about the invocation, not about the strategy.

**What it cost.** 76 records read as FOUND until this was separated out.

**What happens instead.** `WARMUP_NEEDED`, and the ladder supplies a warm-up and runs it.

### A failed trial run

**What it is.** The strategy will not start, or crashes before it trades.

**Why it does not exclude.** No check has seen it, so nothing about it has been judged. Whether the obstacle can be removed is a different question from whether the strategy is out.

**What it cost.** 99 rows sat in `excluded` on this.

**What happens instead.** Status `open`, labelled `to be fixed`, with the obstacle named in `repair_family` and `runtime_failure`. The repair list records what has been recovered this way and what has not.

## Using this list on an unfinished strategy

For a strategy under `open` or `exclusion unconfirmed`, read the three criteria in order and stop at the first that holds.

1. Does `lookahead` read `FOUND` **and** `lookahead_evidence` read `native`? Then C1, and it is excluded.
2. Does `recursive_evidence` read `convergence:not_settled`? Then C2, and it is excluded.
3. Did the full window run and produce no trades, with `trade_evidence` reading `full_window`? Then C3, and it is excluded.
4. Otherwise it is not excluded. `open_work` names what is missing, and the checks run in the order the status page sets out: trial run, recursion, look-ahead, backtest.
